What changes—and what stays yours

Urgency is host-authored input. It changes a row’s influence on the shared field, so existing particles respond around the row’s full bounds. Each reading is the engine’s weighted count of particles near that row, queried from live field state; it does not rank questions or decide which matters.

Select a row, then change its urgency. Compare the live particle samples; connect nearby questions to let their responses interact.
